克拉尼圖形 (Chladni Figures) 原理

克拉尼圖形是由德國物理學家 Ernst Chladni 發現的聲學現象。當金屬板受到特定頻率的振動時，波在板的邊界反射，形成駐波 (Standing Waves)。

駐波產生兩種區域：

  • 波腹 (Antinodes)：振動最劇烈的地方，沙粒會被彈開。
  • 波節 (Nodes / Nodal Lines)：完全不振動的區域。

沙粒最終會沉澱聚集在「波節」上，形成美麗的幾何圖案。其數學方程式可近似表示為：

方形板方程式：

$$ W(x,y) = \cos\left(\frac{n\pi x}{L}\right) \cos\left(\frac{m\pi y}{L}\right) - \cos\left(\frac{m\pi x}{L}\right) \cos\left(\frac{n\pi y}{L}\right) = 0 $$

圓形板方程式：

$$ W(r,\theta) = \sin(n\theta) \cdot J_n(k_m r) = 0 $$

其中 \(n, m\) 為模態階數，決定了節線的數量與形狀。頻率越高，圖案越複雜！

Chladni figures are an acoustic phenomenon discovered by German physicist Ernst Chladni. When a metal plate is vibrated at specific frequencies, waves reflect at the boundaries to form Standing Waves.

Standing waves create two distinct regions:

  • Antinodes: Areas of maximum vibration where sand gets bounced away.
  • Nodes (Nodal Lines): Areas that remain completely stationary.

The sand eventually settles on the "Nodes", forming beautiful geometric patterns. The mathematical equations can be approximated as:

Square Plate Equation:

$$ W(x,y) = \cos\left(\frac{n\pi x}{L}\right) \cos\left(\frac{m\pi y}{L}\right) - \cos\left(\frac{m\pi x}{L}\right) \cos\left(\frac{n\pi y}{L}\right) = 0 $$

Circular Plate Equation:

$$ W(r,\theta) = \sin(n\theta) \cdot J_n(k_m r) = 0 $$

Where \(n, m\) represent the mode integers, determining the number and shape of nodal lines. Higher frequencies result in more complex patterns!
