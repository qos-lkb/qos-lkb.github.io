Why Are Motor Magnets Curved?

Concave Arc Pole Shoes vs. Flat Block Magnets

Flat Block Magnets

• Field lines ($\vec{B}$) are strictly parallel.
• Torque follows: $\tau = N I A B \sin\theta$.
• At vertical position ($\theta = 0^\circ$ or $180^\circ$), torque drops to ZERO (dead-zone!), stalling the motor!

Curved Arc Magnets

• Field lines are RADIAL (pointing to central rotor axis).
• $\vec{B}$ stays perpendicular ($\perp$) to coil wire across the arc angle.
• Delivers constant maximum torque ($\tau = N I A B$) with no dead spots!

Core Physics Principle: Cylindrical concave magnets ensure maximum magnetic flux linkage and smooth continuous motor rotation.

How To Speed Up An Electric Motor

Increasing Lorentz Force ($F = N \cdot I \cdot L \cdot B$) & Torque

1. Increase Voltage ($V$)

Drives higher current ($I = V/R$) through the coil loops, proportional to force.

2. Stronger Magnets ($B$)

Higher magnetic flux density directly multiplies magnetic force on wires.

3. More Coil Loops ($N$)

Adding wire turns stacks individual electromagnetic forces together.

4. Larger Rotor Radius ($R$)

Increases the torque arm length ($\tau = F \times R$) turning the shaft.
