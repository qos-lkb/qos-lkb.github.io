原理解說

概念

氫原子電子只能處在離散定態能階 $E_n=-13.6/n^2\,\mathrm{eV}$。外界以光子或電子供給能量時，原子可被激發到較高 $n$，或在能量足夠時電離；自激發態回落時放出光子（發射）。

機制

  • 光子：必須幾乎精確等於兩能階差 $\Delta E=E_f-E_i$（本模擬容差 0.01 eV）才吸收；否則穿透。
  • 電子：非彈性碰撞——動能 $\ge\Delta E$ 即可激發，剩餘動能由入射電子帶走；不足則彈性。
  • 電離：使電子能量 $\ge 0$（基態約需 13.6 eV）。
  • 假設：僅氫；圓軌道示意；隨機自發發射；非多元素譜線庫、非點箭頭量測 sibling。
註：若電子已在 $n=2$ ($-3.4\text{ eV}$)，只需額外 $3.4\text{ eV}$ 即可電離。

日常例子

氣體放電管／霓虹燈的激發概念（細節指紋見 sibling 發射線模擬）；熒光燈起動時電子碰撞激發；光譜管實驗中「共振波長才吸收」的定性對照。勿與「點能階箭頭讀 ΔE／λ」或「選多氣體看整條發射指紋」混為同一套操作。

1913年，尼爾斯·玻爾提出三個著名公設，成功解釋了氫原子光譜。模型結合了經典力學的圓周運動與量子化的概念。

一、定態公設 (Stationary States)

電子在特定的軌道上繞核運動時，具有固定的能量，且不會輻射電磁波。

$$r_n = n^2 \cdot a_0 \quad (a_0 \approx 0.529 \text{\AA})$$

二、頻率條件 (Frequency Condition)

躍遷時吸收或放出的光子能量恰等於兩能階之差：

$$\Delta E = |E_f - E_i| = h\nu$$

三、角動量量子化 (Angular Momentum)

電子繞核運動的角動量 $L$ 只能是 $\hbar$ 的整數倍：

$$L = mvr = n\hbar \quad (n=1, 2, 3 \dots)$$

Concept

In hydrogen, the electron occupies discrete stationary levels $E_n=-13.6/n^2\,\mathrm{eV}$. An incident photon or electron can excite the atom to higher $n$, or ionize it if enough energy is supplied; falling back emits a photon (emission).

Mechanism

  • Photon: must nearly match $\Delta E=E_f-E_i$ (0.01 eV tolerance here) or it passes through.
  • Electron: inelastic if KE $\ge\Delta E$; leftover KE stays with the projectile; else elastic.
  • Ionization: electron energy $\ge 0$ (~13.6 eV from ground).
  • Idealizations: H only; circular orbits; random spontaneous emission; not a multi-element line library, not the click-arrow sibling.
Note: If the electron is already at $n=2$ ($-3.4\text{ eV}$), only an additional $3.4\text{ eV}$ is needed to ionize.

Everyday example

Discharge／neon excitation intuition (full fingerprints → sibling emission-line sim); electron-impact excitation in fluorescent starters; “only resonant wavelengths absorb” as a qualitative classroom cue. Do not merge with “click transition arrows for one $\Delta E$／λ” or “pick many gases for a full emission fingerprint.”

In 1913, Niels Bohr proposed three famous postulates that successfully explained the Hydrogen spectrum. The model combines classical mechanics' circular motion with the concept of quantization.

1. Stationary States

Electrons orbit the nucleus in specific orbits with fixed energy without radiating electromagnetic waves. These orbits are called Stationary States.

$$r_n = n^2 \cdot a_0 \quad (a_0 \approx 0.529 \text{\AA})$$

2. Frequency Condition

When an electron transitions between energy levels $E_i$ and $E_f$, it must absorb or emit a photon of a specific frequency:

$$\Delta E = |E_f - E_i| = h\nu$$

3. Angular Momentum Quantization

The angular momentum $L$ of the electron orbiting the nucleus can only be integer multiples of $\hbar$ ($h/2\pi$):

$$L = mvr = n\hbar \quad (n=1, 2, 3 \dots)$$
